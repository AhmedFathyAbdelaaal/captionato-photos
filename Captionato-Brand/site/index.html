Plate 02 · CAP-CLR

Palette

Two colors carry the brand; three keep it warm. Red leads, ink grounds, brass is the metal you earn.

Ember Red

The signal. Primary actions, the mark's field, one bold accent per view.

Ink

Warm near-black. Body text, structure, and the felt board pins pop against.

Brass

The keyline metal. Thin rims and small marks only. Precious, never a fill.

The backing card and default ground. Warm, matte, easy on the eyes.
